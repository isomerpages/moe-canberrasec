<h3><strong>2025 VEX V5 Signature Event in Taiwan: Building Robots, Resilience and Teamwork</strong></h3>
<p>From 17 to 20 December 2025, five students from Canberra Secondary School
travelled to Hsinchu, Taiwan, to participate in a VEX V5 Signature Event
held at Pacific American School.</p>
<p>The overseas competition gave our Robotics CCA students the opportunity
to apply their robotics knowledge and skills in an authentic and challenging
environment. Beyond competing, the students gained valuable experience
in teamwork, problem-solving, communication and adapting to unfamiliar
situations.</p>
<h4><strong>Setting Off for Taiwan</strong></h4>
<p>The students began their journey with excitement as they departed Singapore
for Taiwan. Representing both Canberra Secondary School and Singapore,
they understood the responsibility of conducting themselves well and supporting
one another throughout the trip.</p>
<p>Travelling overseas as a team also encouraged the students to become more
independent. They had to manage their belongings, follow the programme
schedule and look out for their teammates.</p>

<h4><strong>Arriving at Pacific American School</strong></h4>
<p>The competition was held at Pacific American School in Hsinchu. Upon arriving
at the venue, the students familiarised themselves with the competition
environment and prepared their equipment for the event.</p>
<p>Participating in an overseas competition exposed the students to a different
setting and a larger community of robotics enthusiasts. It gave them the
opportunity to observe how other teams approached robot design, preparation
and competition strategy.</p>

<h4><strong>Preparing the Robot</strong></h4>
<p>Careful preparation was essential throughout the competition. The students
worked together to inspect the robot, check its mechanisms and make the
necessary adjustments before their matches.</p>
<p>When problems arose, they had to identify the possible causes, discuss
solutions and carry out repairs within a limited amount of time. Every
student contributed to the preparation process by sharing ideas, completing
assigned tasks and supporting the team.</p>
<p>These moments reminded the students that robotics is not only about constructing
and programming a robot. It also requires patience, perseverance, careful
observation and effective communication.</p>

<h4><strong>Taking Part in the VEX V5 Competition</strong></h4>
<p>During the competition, the students put their preparation into action.
They had to communicate clearly, respond quickly to changing situations
and make strategic decisions during each match.</p>
<p>The competition also allowed them to observe a variety of robot designs
and strategies used by other teams. By interacting with and learning from
fellow participants, the students gained new perspectives and ideas that
could support their future development in robotics.</p>
<p>Regardless of the results of each match, the team remained focused and
continued to reflect on how the robot and their approach could be improved.</p>

<h4><strong>Sharing Their Engineering Process</strong></h4>
<p>As part of the competition experience, the students participated in an
interview where they shared their robot design and engineering process.</p>
<p>They explained the ideas behind the robot, the roles of different team
members, the challenges encountered and the improvements made during their
preparation. Responding to questions required the students to think carefully
and communicate their ideas clearly.</p>
<p>The interview helped them understand that engineers must not only create
solutions but also explain and justify the decisions made throughout the
design process.</p>

<h4><strong>Growing Through the Experience</strong></h4>
<p>The trip provided many learning opportunities beyond the competition itself.
The students learned to remain calm when faced with unexpected problems,
persevere when their initial solutions did not work and support one another
during demanding moments.</p>
<p>They also developed greater confidence and independence through travelling
overseas, managing their responsibilities and representing the school in
an unfamiliar environment.</p>
<p>Most importantly, the students learned that progress in robotics comes
through continuous testing, reflection and improvement. Challenges and
setbacks became opportunities for the team to learn and grow together.</p>

<h4><strong>A Memorable Learning Journey</strong></h4>
<p>The PAS Taiwan Robotics Trip was more than an opportunity to participate
in an overseas competition. It was a meaningful learning journey that strengthened
the students’ technical skills, resilience, teamwork and confidence.</p>
<p>The lessons learned and memories created in Hsinchu will continue to inspire
the five students as they take on future challenges in robotics and beyond.</p>
